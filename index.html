How It Works

About the Game

3D Bricks Slider Game is a fast-paced game that challenges players' precision, reflexes, and ability to avoid hazards.

Game Objective & Controls

Earn points by swiping regular bricks with your mouse or touchscreen as they appear. Progress through the game by continuously scoring and surviving.

⚠️ Bomb Warning! ⚠️

Bombs may appear alongside regular bricks during gameplay. Do not swipe bombs! Swiping a bomb immediately ends the current game. Stay alert and avoid them.

Discover New Worlds

Every 500 points, the game's background will dynamically evolve into a new, unique environment. See how far you can progress and unlock them all!
