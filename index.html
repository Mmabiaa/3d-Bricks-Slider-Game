How It Works

🎯 Objective

Swipe bricks off the screen to score points. You start with 3 lives (❤️❤️❤️). Avoid mistakes to survive as long as possible and discover beautiful new environments.

🕹️ Controls & Mechanics

👆 Swipe a brick Score points + splash effect
🟦 Swipe a wireframe Activates Slow-Motion mode
💣 Swipe a bomb Lose 1 Life ❤️
❌ Miss a brick (Ranked) Lose 1 Life ❤️
➖ Miss a brick (Casual) −25 Points
⏸️ Press 'P' Pause / Resume
🔊 HUD Button Toggle Sound Effects

💣 Hazards: Bombs

Dark bombs randomly spawn alongside regular bricks. Do not swipe them! Swiping a bomb triggers an explosion and costs one life. Bombs that fall safely off-screen will not harm you.

🎮 Game Modes

⚔️ Ranked

Mistakes cost lives. High scores are saved locally.

😎 Casual

Missing bricks deducts 25 points. Relax and have fun!

🌍 Progression & Worlds

Every 100 points (Ranked) or 20 bricks (Casual), you advance a Level and unlock a vibrant new environment. There are 10 unique worlds that endlessly cycle forward.
