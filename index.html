How It Works

🎮 About the Game

3D Bricks Slider Game is a fast-paced, reflex-driven arcade game. Swipe bricks off the screen to score points, survive as long as possible, and discover beautiful new environments as you progress.

🕹️ Controls

👆 Swipe a brick Score points + splash effect
💣 Swipe a bomb Instant Game Over!
🟦 Swipe a wireframe brick Triggers Slow-Motion
❌ Miss a brick (Ranked) Game Over
➖ Miss a brick (Casual) −25 points
⏸️ Press P (keyboard) Pause / Resume
🔊 Sound button (HUD) Toggle sound on/off

⚠️ Bomb Warning!

Bombs randomly appear alongside regular bricks. Never swipe a bomb — doing so triggers a dramatic explosion and immediately ends your game. Bombs that fall off screen are safe and won't harm you.

🎮 Game Modes

⚔️ Ranked

Miss a brick and it's over. High scores are saved.

😎 Casual

Miss a brick and lose 25pts. No pressure, just fun.

🌍 Dynamic Backgrounds

Every 100 points the game environment transforms into a vivid new world — from Teal to Midnight Blue to Deep Crimson and beyond. There are 10 unique environments that cycle infinitely, so the colours never stop evolving.

🔊 Sound Effects

Every action has satisfying audio feedback — a splash when you smash a brick, an explosion when a bomb detonates, a transition sound when a new world unlocks, and a confirmation when you start. Toggle sound anytime via the 🔊 button in the top-right corner.
